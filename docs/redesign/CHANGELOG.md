# What's new in APEX DMS — the new look

*For everyone at APEX Trading Co. who uses the DMS.*

## It looks calmer
- Pages are light grey and white, with **one blue** for everything you can press. Other colours now only mean a
  status: amber = waiting, green = done/paid, red = overdue or short, teal = on the way. Every status also has an icon
  and a word.
- Text is a little larger (nothing smaller than 12px) and dates read like **17-Nov-2026**.
- Big amounts on the dashboard show as **₹10.61 Cr** or **₹10.17 L**, so they are never cut off on a phone. Hold the
  pointer on a figure (or open it) to see the exact amount.

## Finding things is faster
- **Ctrl K** (⌘ K on a Mac), or the search box at the top: type an invoice number, shop name, phone number or product
  model and press Enter. It also opens any page and starts common tasks.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** (when you are not typing in a box):

  | Key | Does |
  | --- | --- |
  | I | New invoice |
  | N | New order / quotation |
  | P | Record payment |
  | / | Search |
  | G then D | Dashboard |
  | G then S | Billing |
  | G then O | Invoices |
  | G then R | Retailers |
  | G then K | Inventory |
  | G then P | Payments |
  | G then X | Dispatch |
  | ? | Show all shortcuts |

  The billing screen keeps its own keys: F4 retailer, / product search, F2 catalogue, Ctrl Enter generate invoice.
- The **menu** is grouped (Sales, Inventory, Dispatch, Accounts, Reports, Settings). The **pin** button at the top adds
  the page you are on to the menu; recently opened invoices and shops appear there too. The button at the bottom of
  the menu folds it down to icons.
- On a **phone**, the office app has a bottom bar with your four most-used pages; **More** opens the full menu.

## Dashboard
- **Needs attention** comes first: overdue money, collections to verify, invoices waiting for dispatch and low stock.
  Each one opens the right page. When there is nothing to do it says "All clear".

## Billing
- **Quotations save themselves.** As soon as a quotation has a retailer and a product it is saved as a real
  quotation (one per brand), and every change updates the same one; "Saved" shows next to its number. Going back,
  refreshing or opening another page no longer loses it: it is in Quotations. Removing every product of a brand
  cancels that brand's quotation; **Cancel quotation** cancels the whole thing.
- **Three steps, like Odoo: quotation → invoice draft → confirm.** Billing (now "New quotation") only saves a
  quotation; there is no button that makes an invoice straight away. On the quotation, **Create invoice** opens the
  invoice draft: the bill-to details, invoice and due dates, the lines with stock, the GST-details, short-stock and
  credit checks, and the payment received now. **Confirm invoice** issues it and gives it its number. Leaving the
  draft changes nothing; the quotation stays as it was. Salesmen still only send quotations to the office.
- If a product is **short**, a red box appears in the cart straight away with how many you need, how many are in
  stock and how many are short. **Use what's in stock** lowers the quantities for you. The rule is the same as before:
  an order ships in full, and only a manager can invoice anyway as a back-order.
- **Clear cart** now clears at once; press **Undo** in the message if it was a mistake.

## Salesman app
- New look with a frosted bottom bar. Taking an order shows a 3-step bar (Outlet → Products → Review).
- When you reach your monthly target, there is a small celebration — once.

## Your display settings
Open **My account → Display** (salesmen: **More → Account & display**):
- **Theme:** Light (default), Dark, or Same as device. The sun/moon button at the top switches quickly.
- **Table density:** Comfortable or **Compact** (more rows on the screen).
- **Motion:** Full or **Reduce motion** (animations become quick fades). If your phone or computer is set to reduce
  motion, the app follows it.

These settings are saved on each device.

## What did not change
Your data, your login, what each role can see and do, the order and invoice numbers, and all the rules (full
dispatch, credit checks, approvals) are exactly as before.
